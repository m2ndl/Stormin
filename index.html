Topic 1

ESP / Legal English dissertation

Aim

Build a Saudi-context Legal English needs map and convert it into a teachable, assessable framework.

Data
Survey + interviews with Saudi legal professionals; Saudi legal corpus (official translated laws and approved texts).
Design
  • Needs analysis (drivers, tasks/contexts, learning preferences).
  • Corpus-informed language patterns for key legal functions/genres.
  • Framework + prototype syllabus/rubrics, validated by experts and/or a short pilot.
Outputs
Task map + language specification + Saudi Legal English framework (levels, competencies, assessment tools).
Topic 2

MBTI awareness as intervention

Aim

Test whether learning one’s MBTI type (officially administered + debriefed) improves metacognitive strategy use and affects motivation, anxiety, and self-efficacy; explore learner-identity talk.

Data
60–90 Saudi EFL university students (2–3 intact classes).
Design
  • Pre–post mixed methods: baseline scales + MBTI.
  • 1–2 type-awareness workshops + reflections.
  • End-of-term re-test + subset interviews.
Outputs
Evidence of pre–post shifts in strategies/affect + qualitative account of how learners use “type” to explain and adjust learning.
